Senior Customer Success Manager

Hire a human, augment with an agent
Overall score: 64/100

This role blends deep customer empathy, executive-level relationship management, and judgment-heavy renewal negotiations with a long tail of repetitive operational work — health-check reviews, QBR prep, churn-signal triage. A human lead is essential for the qualitative direction-setting and trust-building, but a Lua agent can reliably take 40–60% of the operational load and free the human to focus on the high-stakes moments.
